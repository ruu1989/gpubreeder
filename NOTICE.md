# Notices

## Rust Breeder

GPUBreeder exists because of [Rust Breeder](https://rustbreeder.com) by Wojciech Słodziak
([source](https://github.com/FlareFlo/rust-breeder), [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Va5475pqFw)).
Rust Breeder worked out the problem, showed what a good answer looks like, and has been the answer
key for every part of this project:

- The two example plant lists from rustbreeder.com are used as test inputs in
  `src/selftest.rs` and `tests/site_parity.rs`.
- `tests/site_parity.rs` checks GPUBreeder against results read from the live site, version 3.0.4.
- The scanner was tested against clone lists that Rust Breeder's own scanner produced.
- The paste format, the gene letters and the idea of scanning clones from the screen all follow
  Rust Breeder.
